## Problem & solution

Recruiters and hiring managers screening for senior/staff frontend roles spend seconds per candidate. A CV PDF and LinkedIn list tasks and titles, but do not show that Rayhan makes product and business decisions, not just UI. The v1 site (archived at release/v1) told a broad 'does everything' story, leaned on heavy structure (8 sections, i18n, blog, vendored Prima UI) and its visual design did not represent him.

A fast, single-page personal site that positions Ahmed Rayhan Primadedas as a product-minded senior frontend engineer: outcome-first proof (business impact per project), a short experience summary, and a clear path to CV download and contact. Built to be read by humans and by AI search engines (SEO + GEO).

## Vision

Seven years of frontend work spanning startup speed and national scale: led frontend at a US-based edtech startup (Anify, cut hosting costs 71% via CI/CD), built national-scale healthcare systems for UNDP and Indonesia's Ministry of Health, and led a 1,800+ member developer community (GDG Depok). Few candidates can show outcomes at both ends of that range.

## Who the user is

First reader: recruiters and hiring managers screening for Senior/Staff Frontend Engineer (individual contributor) roles. Leadership (Anify team, mentoring, community) is shown as a strength, not the headline. Secondary: freelance clients and the dev community.
